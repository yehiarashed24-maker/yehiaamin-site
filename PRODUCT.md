# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers looking at my portfolio to evaluate me for a role.

## Product Purpose
It showcases my personal projects and skills with a unique 3D/interactive experience.

## Positioning
It leverages modern web technologies (3D/interactivity) to stand out from standard portfolios, demonstrating advanced frontend capabilities.

## Operating Context
Viewed primarily on desktop and mobile browsers during the hiring and evaluation process.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the React Three Fiber and Framer Motion stack. The 3D interactive aesthetic is required and must be maintained in future work.

## Evidence on Hand
React Three Fiber and Framer Motion are already established in the dependencies and codebase.

## Product Principles
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Use interactivity and 3D elements to demonstrate technical skill rather than just listing it.
- **Performance Matters**: Despite the 3D aesthetic, it must load reliably and smoothly for recruiters.
- **Stand Out**: Be memorable compared to standard PDF resumes or flat websites.
